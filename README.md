## Focus areas

- **Robotics and control:** kinematic and dynamic modelling of manipulators and mobile robots, closed-loop joint control and inverse kinematics on ROS 2 (Nav2, Gazebo, CoppeliaSim).
- **Digital twins and Real-to-Sim:** parameter identification from real robot data and simulator calibration on OpenUSD and NVIDIA Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab, with PyTorch, NumPy and SciPy.
- **Vision and 3D perception:** real-time 3D hand and body pose estimation with MediaPipe and OpenCV; gesture-based teleoperation and hand-eye calibration.
- **Applied AI and agents:** language-model assistants that use custom tools through MCP, with cloud and local models; daily work with coding agents (Claude Code, Antigravity): writing their instructions, splitting up the tasks and verifying what they deliver. Python, C++, C, Dart and SQL; FastAPI, Flutter, Docker, Linux; microcontroller programming.
